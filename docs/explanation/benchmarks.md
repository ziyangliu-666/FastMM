# Benchmarks

Results: [`bench/README.md`](../../bench/README.md), generated by `scripts/bench.sh`.

## Method

- `scripts/bench.sh --preset release-native --cpu 2` pins each benchmark to one core (`taskset` plus `sched_setaffinity` in the bench main), runs 5 repetitions and reports the median.
- `BM_TickToOrder_Sim` and the reactor benchmarks also record each round in a `LogLinearHistogram` and export `p50`/`p99` counters; for `BM_TickToOrder_Sim` they cover the ticks that sent orders. Tables give the median of 5 repetitions unless a figure is marked p50.
- `BM_TickToOrder_Sim` measures the engine only: a book delta into the feed, `Engine::step()`, and the orders serialised into `SimTransport`. TLS, JSON decoding and the network are outside it.
- Between timed ticks the rig runs the simulated venue and delivers acks, untimed, until no order is in flight and both quotes rest at the venue. The quote manager does not touch a pending order, so a tick that finds one sends nothing. Each timed tick moves the book by two ticks and sends one cancel per side. The benchmark reports `order_events_pct`, the share of ticks that sent orders, and fails when it is below 50 %. The median time per iteration includes Google Benchmark's pause and resume overhead around the untimed part; the `p50`/`p99` counters do not.
- `bench/ci_budget.toml` holds p50 budgets and counter floors. `python3 tools/check_budgets.py bench/results/latest` exits 1 when a median exceeds its budget by more than 25 %, a benchmark reported an error, or a median counter is below its `[min_counters]` floor. It reports benchmarks missing from the results without failing; CI does not run it. `tools/bench_compare.py` diffs two result directories.

## Caveats

The results in this repository were measured under WSL2 (Linux 6.6) with no CPU isolation. The hypervisor and other processes add scheduling jitter to every run. The median of 5 repetitions is reported with its standard deviation; p99 counters include the jitter. Pages whose numbers were measured while other builds were running give the load.
